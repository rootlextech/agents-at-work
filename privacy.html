Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

The "Agents at Work Uploader" app accesses YouTube data only for the Agents at Work channel, with the channel owner's permission. It uploads and manages the channel's own videos, replies to comments on those videos, and reads the channel's analytics.

Public comments on our videos and commenters' public display names are processed only to write replies. We do not sell, share or use this data for advertising, and we do not access any other user's account.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube data is accessed through YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time at Google account permissions.
